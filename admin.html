Add photos from my phone

Go to Photos, choose Gallery or Collages, then tap Choose photos. Pick pictures from your phone and wait for the bar to finish.

Change a caption or the order

Type in the caption box under any photo and tap outside it. Use ◀ ▶ to move it. Use 🗑 to delete it.

Write a blog post

Go to Blog, fill the title, add a story and/or a link, add a cover image, then Save post.

Add social media

Go to Social, paste your profile links, then press Save social links. Icons appear at the bottom of the website.

Tip

Add this page to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.
