Security

Every time you sign in you pass 3 checks: email, password and, when it is on, the face lock. You are signed out after 10 minutes of no activity.

Face lock off

After your email and password, the camera asks you to look straight, then turn your head left and right. It is an extra lock, not a replacement for your password. The camera picture is never saved, only a small face "fingerprint".
