Help

Everything in plain words.

Add photos to a place

Go to Photos. Each box has a name, like "Main gallery" or "Spotlight". Tap Add photos inside the box you want and pick pictures from your phone.

Hide a photo or a whole place

Tap the 👁 button under a photo to hide it. To hide a whole box, use its Show on website switch, or go to the Show tab to switch any part of the website on or off.

Hide or move many photos

Tick the small boxes on several photos. A bar appears at the bottom to hide, show, move or delete them all at once.

I deleted something by mistake

Open Trash and tap Restore. Nothing is gone until you delete it forever.

Chat

Visitors see a chat bubble on the website. Their messages appear in the Chat tab and you can reply there. Tap "Turn on alerts" to get a sound and a pop-up.

Tip

Add this page to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.
